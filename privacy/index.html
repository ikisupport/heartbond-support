Privacy Policy

This page describes how HeartBond handles your health data.

HeartBond reads health data from Apple Health and stores your downloaded days in your own iCloud account. There is no HeartBond account and no HeartBond server holding that data.

Consent

The app follows Apple's privacy guidelines and asks for your explicit consent before reading health data. You can review or withdraw that access in the Health app at any time.

What is read

HeartBond requests permission to read heart rate, heart rate variability (SDNN), heartbeat series, and sleep analysis. It graphs heart rate and HRV, and draws each night's sleep stages behind them. It does not write samples back to Health.

HealthKit

On iPhone and iPad, health data is read through Apple's HealthKit framework, which governs how that information is requested, stored, and protected on the device. A Mac reads Health only when the system reports it as available. Otherwise it shows days that arrived over iCloud.

iCloud sync

The app follows Apple's iCloud guidelines and uses iCloud only for content you choose to sync across your own devices. Downloaded sample days, Keep all samples markers, and per-device graph colors travel through your iCloud account. Graph size and most display preferences stay on that device.

Security

Health data is encrypted in transit while it moves between your devices through iCloud.

App Store guidelines

HeartBond follows the App Store Review Guidelines that apply to health, privacy, safety, and functionality.

Medical disclaimer

HeartBond is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Regional rules

Health data laws differ by territory. The app is built to meet the rules that apply where it is offered.

This website

This support site carries no analytics or tracking scripts. If that changes, this section will say what is collected and by whom.
